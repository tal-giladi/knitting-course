# Module 3 quiz

Eight scenario questions about the purl stitch, about stockinette, and about why knitted fabrics
curl. Most of them describe something you could hold in your hands — a swatch, a scarf that will
not lie flat, a hat that fits — and ask what is going on.

**Pass mark: 70%.** You need 6 of 8. Read the explanation even when you get it right: the point of
this module is a rule you can apply later, not a set of facts. The
[glossary](../glossary.md) links every term to the lesson that introduces it.

Review the module first if you need it:

- [03.1 · The purl stitch](../lessons/module-03/lesson-01.md)
- [03.2 · Stockinette and why it curls](../lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)

If you made the two-swatch comparison in lesson 03.2, keep it beside you. Several questions here are
much easier to answer with the physical result in front of you than on paper.
