# Module 1 quiz

Eight questions on the material in Module 1: what a knitted stitch actually is, how to choose your
first yarn and needles, how to hold them, and the cast-on. The questions are situations, not
definitions. You will be given something that happened or something you are about to do, and asked
what it means and what to do next. The pass mark is 70%, so six of the eight.

**What this quiz covers:**

- **Loops pulled through loops** — one strand, anchored only below, not locked, two faces. Most of
  the other questions rest on this one.
- **Choosing yarn and needles** — why smooth, light-coloured, worsted-weight yarn is the beginner's
  default, and what to do when the yarn you have is not that.
- **Reading the label** — weight, fibre, ply, yardage, recommended needle size and dye lot.
- **Holding yarn and needles** — English and Continental, and where tension really comes from.
- **The slip knot and the cast-on** — why the bottom edge of a knitted piece is just the first row
  of loops, and why it can be taken off and done again.
