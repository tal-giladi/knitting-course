# Module 2 quiz

Eight scenario questions about tension, the shape of a stitch, and the mistakes that show up in
your first few rows of knitting. None of them asks you to remember a term; each one describes
something you can hold in your hands and asks what happened.

**Pass mark: 70%.** You need 6 of 8. Every explanation says why the right answer is right, so a
wrong answer is usually worth more than a right one you guessed. The [Glossary](../glossary.md)
links every term to the lesson that introduces it.

Review the module first if you need it:

- [02.1 · The knit stitch](../lessons/module-02/lesson-01.md)

Your [swatch card](../templates/swatch-card.md) is worth having beside you for the first
question: several of these are things you will be able to check on your own fabric, not just
recognise on paper.
