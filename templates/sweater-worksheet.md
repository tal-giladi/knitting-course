# Sweater worksheet — your gauge, your size

Everything you need to plan the sweater, in the order you need it. Write in the boxes, do the
arithmetic on a calculator, and check each step before moving on. This worksheet is the difference
between a sweater that fits and a sweater that fits a different person from you.

Your measurements come from lesson 11.1; the geometry is in lesson 11.3.

## 3 · Ease

Ease is what you add to your own measurement. Pick it deliberately; the default in a boxy sweater

## 4 · Turn measurements into stitch counts

$$\text{stitches} = \frac{\text{finished circumference} \times \text{S}}{10}$$

$$\text{rounds} = \frac{\text{length} \times \text{R}}{10}$$

| | Calculation | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Body cast-on** (1x1 rib) | ______ cm × S ÷ 10 | ______ sts |
| **Raglan yoke depth** | ______ cm × R ÷ 10 | ______ rounds |
| **Body length, underarm to hem** | ______ cm × R ÷ 10 | ______ rounds |
| **Sleeve length, underarm to wrist** | ______ cm × R ÷ 10 | ______ rounds |
| **Hem rib rounds** | usually 5-8 cm | ______ rounds |
| **Neckband rib rounds** | usually 5-8 cm | ______ rounds |

**Stitches per 4 lines before the first increase round:**

$$\text{raglan section} = \frac{\text{cast-on} - \text{neckband stitches} - \text{magic loop stitches}}{4}$$

| | |
|---|---|
| Cast-on | ______ sts |
| Neckband (1x1 rib is half the cast-on) | ______ sts |
| Magic-loop adjustment | ______ sts |
| **Per section** | ______ sts |

The number must be **even**, so each of the four lines starts with a whole pair of stitches. If it
is odd, add or remove one stitch and say so in your notes — this is a normal adjustment, not a
mistake.

## 6 · Before you cast on — checks

- [ ] My gauge was measured **after** washing, not before.
- [ ] My cast-on is **even** (ribbing needs an even number).
- [ ] My section stitch count is **even**, so the 4 lines line up.
- [ ] I have a **lifeline** and a **row counter**.
- [ ] I will **try the yoke on** at the underarm, and every 10 cm after.
- [ ] I have more yarn than I need, in **one dye lot**.
