# Templates

Printable and fill-in worksheets used by the course. Each module's copy of the worksheets it needs
is also in that module's lab download, so you can print them without leaving the Academy.

| Template | Use it |
|---|---|
| [Swatch card](swatch-card.md) | Once per practice swatch. The swatch book is the point: you are building a physical library of fabrics and gauges, and this card is the index. |
| [Project notes](project-notes.md) | Once per project — scarf, hat, sweater, and your own design. The last section is the one that matters most: what you would change next time. |
| [Sweater worksheet](sweater-worksheet.md) | When you plan the sweater: your measurements, your gauge, and the stitch counts that follow from them. |
| [Pattern template](pattern-template.md) | Module 13, when you write your own pattern. Also the structure this course's own patterns use. |

## How to use the swatch card

Make one card per swatch, write the details while the swatch is still on the needles, and keep both
together. By Module 6 you will have around a dozen swatches, and they become the answer to
questions like "what does seed stitch look like at 4 mm?" and "how much does this yarn grow after
washing?" without any further knitting.

If you have an envelope or a card index, file them. If you have a camera, photograph each finished
swatch and keep the photo with the card. Both work; the only version that does not work is the one
where you throw the swatch out.
