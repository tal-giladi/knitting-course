## Summary

Learn to knit from your very first stitch to a scarf, a hat and a sweater that fits, and
understand why knitting works so you can fix, adapt and design on your own.

## Who it is for

Complete beginners, adults or teenagers, who have never knitted (or forgot what they learned
as a child) and want to make real, wearable things. Left-handed knitters are supported
throughout.

## Format

66 short illustrated lessons in 14 modules: numbered steps, a diagram for every motion, short
technique videos, a practice swatch in almost every lesson, and multiple-choice quizzes after
each lesson and module. Four practical projects: a scarf, a hat, a simple top-down sweater,
and a hat you design yourself. About 10-12 hours of reading and 50-80 hours of knitting.

## Prerequisites

None. You need one 5 mm (US 8) circular needle with an 80 cm cable, smooth light-coloured
worsted-weight wool, scissors, a tapestry needle and a tape measure. Basic arithmetic with a
calculator for gauge and sizing.

## What you will learn

- Cast on, knit, purl, bind off, and knit flat and in the round with even tension.
- How knitted fabric is built, and why stockinette curls, ribbing stretches and garter lies flat.
- Knit and measure a gauge swatch, and use gauge to control the size of everything you make.
- Choose and substitute yarn by weight and fibre, and predict how it will behave.
- Read your own knitting and fix mistakes: tinking, laddering down, frogging to a lifeline.
- Read written patterns, schematics and charts, and choose the right size using ease.
- Knit a scarf, a hat and a top-down raglan sweater that fits you.
- Design a simple hat from your own gauge and write a pattern others can follow.
