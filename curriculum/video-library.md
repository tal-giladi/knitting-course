# Verified video library (production reference)

Never imported; learners see only the links embedded in the lessons. Every URL below was checked
against the YouTube oEmbed endpoint (`.../oembed?url=...&format=json`) on 2026-10-01 and returned a
title and an author, so it embeds. Titles and channels are copied verbatim from that response.

**Rules for using these links**

- One YouTube URL **alone on its own line** in a lesson becomes an embedded player.
- Never paste an `<iframe>` (it is stripped on import).
- The line before the video says what it adds. Video is support: the text and diagram must be
  complete without it.
- Attribute by channel name, not by person, so attribution stays accurate if a creator changes it.
- Deep-link into a long video with `&t=NNNs` when only one chapter is relevant.
- The two broken IDs to avoid: `FVSwjIUiQZlM` (a typo of the bind-off video) and `FJb5NBK1NY64`.

## Lessons with no video, and what to do instead

No working video was found for these. **Do not substitute a video that is not in the table above.**
Teach them with text, a table and a diagram, and say nothing about video in the lesson.

| Lesson | Why it works without one |
|---|---|
| 05.3 tinking, 05.5 frogging and lifelines | No verified video exists. Undoing is best taught as a slow step sequence with a before/after diagram, because the video shows the *result* better than the *cause*. |
| 02.3 garter stitch | No standalone video. It is "knit every row" — one sentence, one fabric comparison diagram. |
| 10.3 reading charts | No beginner chart tutorial. Teach it with a real sample chart and a worked round; a 48-minute project video is worse than none. |
| 11.3 raglan increases | Only the *cast-on* has a video. The increase rounds are pure geometry — a diagram plus the count table is clearer. |
| 10.1 anatomy of a pattern, 10.5 when a pattern seems wrong | Judgement, not motion. Use the worked example in the lesson. |

## When you need more videos

1. Fetch the channel RSS feed: `https://www.youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=UC...`
   — real IDs, titles and descriptions in plain XML, and descriptions cross-link dozens of related
   technique videos. Known channel IDs: Sheep & Stitch `UCXK_Yw8hCF-9oeccQP9Gs2g`,
   NimbleNeedles - Two `UCDUgpVfqgFHcFGq-AkhktoQ`, VeryPink Knits `UCrRT4LwemxYrZxj-HeCkK9Q`,
   Jessica McDonald `UCqeL3IW8l11a90Trt59F8Cg`, West Yorkshire Spinners
   `UC1gLInmxjkseo4Tb7eF4iUw`. It only exposes the ~15 most recent uploads, so older classics are
   unreachable this way.
2. **Verify every candidate** against the oEmbed endpoint before it goes in a lesson. A dead embed
   is a visible defect and the worst outcome of this policy.
3. Note that these sites lazy-load their iframes, so fetching them yields no IDs: nimble-needles.com,
   purlsoho.com (403), threadandmaple.com, woolandthegang.com, brooklyntweed.com.
